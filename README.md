## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital farmer-to-buyer marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, restaurants, and bulk buyers.

The platform will allow farmers to list their available agricultural products, quantity, quality, expected price, and location. Buyers can directly view products and place orders without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The system will also provide AI-based demand forecasting to predict the demand for different agricultural products. A logistics module will help identify suitable transportation options and optimize delivery routes.

The main goal is to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve farmers' earnings, reduce consumer prices, minimize post-harvest losses, and make the agricultural supply chain more efficient.

How It Addresses the Problem

Direct connection between farmers/FPOs and buyers.

Transparent product and price information.

AI-based demand prediction to reduce overproduction and wastage.

Logistics and route optimization for efficient transportation.

Bulk-order support for retailers and other large buyers.

Digital tracking of orders from farmer to customer.

Innovation and Uniqueness

The uniqueness of the platform is the combination of a digital marketplace, AI demand forecasting, and intelligent logistics management in a single system. Instead of focusing only on buying and selling, the solution addresses important supply-chain problems such as demand uncertainty, transportation costs, and unnecessary intermediaries.

## Technologies to Be Used

## Feasibility and Viability
## Feasibility

The proposed solution is technically feasible because it can be developed using widely available web technologies, databases, cloud services, AI/ML frameworks, and mapping APIs.

The platform can initially be implemented as a prototype for selected farmers/FPOs and buyers and later expanded to different regions.

## Potential Challenges and Risks

- Limited digital literacy among some farmers.
- Difficulty in obtaining reliable demand and pricing data.
- Transportation and delivery challenges in rural areas.
- Product quality and quantity verification.
- Internet connectivity problems in some rural locations.

## Strategies to Overcome Challenges

- Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.
- Offer training and support for farmers and FPOs.
- Partner with local logistics providers.
- Implement product quality and seller verification.
- Provide alternative communication/support mechanisms for areas with limited connectivity.
- Continuously improve AI predictions using updated market and order data.
## Impact and Benefits
# Impact and Benefits

## Potential Impact

The proposed platform can create a more transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain by reducing unnecessary intermediaries and connecting producers directly with buyers.

It can help farmers receive better returns for their products while enabling consumers and bulk buyers to access agricultural products at more competitive prices.

## Benefits

### Economic Benefits
- Improved income opportunities for farmers.
- Reduced intermediary margins.
- Lower transportation and supply-chain costs.
- Better price transparency.

### Social Benefits
- Direct digital access to markets for farmers and FPOs.
- Better participation of rural communities in digital commerce.
- Improved communication between producers and buyers.

### Environmental Benefits
- Reduced unnecessary transportation.
- Optimized delivery routes can reduce fuel consumption.
- Better demand forecasting can help reduce food wastage.
- More efficient movement of agricultural products.

### Overall Benefit

The solution aims to create a **transparent, farmer-friendly, technology-driven agricultural marketplace** that benefits farmers, consumers, bulk buyers, and logistics providers.
